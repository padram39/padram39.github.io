① NAVIGATION

Six orphan pages collapsed into one scroll ordered by intent — see the room, read the menu, book a table. Nothing lives more than one swipe from the reservation form.

② TYPOGRAPHY

System Times at 12px became Cormorant display over a 16px Lora body at 1.6 line-height — the menu now reads like the printed one on the tables.

③ TRUST

The visitor counter and broken JPEGs gave way to the things diners actually verify: photography, opening hours, an address that maps, prices on the page.

④ ACTION

“Chiamateci!” (phone only, at mealtimes) became a booking form above the fold — with the phone number kept one tap away for the regulars who prefer it.

AUDIT

Fixed 900px table layout, unreadable on phones — where 78% of “restaurant near me” traffic lives. No prices, no hours on the homepage, booking possible only by phone at mealtimes.

STRATEGY

One page, photography-led, in a warm system built from the room itself: cream walls, olive shutters, terracotta floor. The menu becomes content, not a .doc download. Booking becomes a form with instant confirmation.

OUTCOME (ILLUSTRATIVE)

Mobile bounce −41%. Reservation requests +38%, half arriving outside opening hours — bookings the phone-only era simply never heard. Menu downloads: zero, because it's on the page.
